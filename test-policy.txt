# Company Vacation Policy

## Overview
Employees are entitled to 15 days of paid vacation per year.

## Eligibility
- Full-time employees: 15 days
- Part-time employees: Prorated based on hours worked

## Requesting Time Off
Submit requests at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.
